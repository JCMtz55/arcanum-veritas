Abilities

Dream is the seventh score. It starts at 10 and only a campaign event raises it, so it is the DM's to set — never point buy, never an ASI.

Saving Throws

Combat

Wounds & Injuries

Each active Wound and each unhealed Injury adds +1 to both your death-save DC and your concentration DC. At the sixth Wound the DM determines an Injury, and Wounds reset to the number of active Injuries, capped at five.

Exhaustion

Two separate tracks. Standard Exhaustion is 2014's; Dream Exhaustion runs 0–8, is cumulative, and its d20 penalty applies in dream only.

Honor & Morality

Four independent sub-scores. The total is the sum; a situational check uses the difference, positive when the dominant side matches the situation and negative when it does not. Every DM-granted level issues one Honor and one Morality point to spend.

Fate Die — Rolling With Emphasis

Charges are granted by the DM alone and nothing recharges them. Roll two d20 and keep the face farthest from 10; equidistant faces reroll both.

Attunement

Three baseline slots, and three more in Insomnia for Dreamlike and Lucidlike items. Leaving Insomnia suspends what those three grant — it never unattunes them.

Skills

Cognitive Sense

A Dream Ability, usable in Insomnia only, after a minute of focus. One use per short rest, two at Dream 18 or higher — and Pulse and Whisper spend from the same pool.
