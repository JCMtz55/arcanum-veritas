# Arcanum Veritas Composition

_The **Ring** of a seal decides its shape and scale. Choose one Composition and subtype for every seal you draw. Rules, limits, countering, and the manners of drawing live in [[Arcanum Veritas RE|Arcanum Veritas]]; this note is the tables._

**One Seal, One Roll.** Whatever the Ring, a seal asks each target for at most one d20 — the seal attack roll, or one saving throw whose ability is set by the **Keystone**. Sigils never add a saving throw of their own, and a condition a Sigil imposes lasts only until the end of the target's next turn.

---

## Shape

Every Ring with a radius — **Area**, **Field**, and **Control — Area** — may be drawn in one of three shapes. Same dice, same slot; only the geometry changes.

|Shape|Origin|Dimensions|
|---|---|---|
|**Sphere**|a point within range|radius as tabled|
|**Cone**|you|length = 2× the tabled radius|
|**Line**|you|length = 4× the tabled radius, 5 ft wide|

_Cones and lines have no range — they begin at your hand._

|---|---|---|

|2nd|3d8 + VM|90 ft|
|3rd|4d8 + VM|120 ft|

_Direct Attack is the precise strike — no save, guaranteed Verum on a hit, and it can crit. It never hits more than one creature; that is what Area is for._

**Touch variant.** Draw the seal into your hand instead: range 5 ft, and the damage gains **one additional die**.

## Offensive — Infusion

_Draw the seal into a held weapon. Every hit deals bonus damage of the Keystone's type and carries its Verum._

|Slot|Weapon Die|Duration|
|---|---|---|
|1st|1d6|until end of your next turn|
|2nd|1d6|1 minute|
|3rd|1d8|1 minute|
|4th|1d8|10 minutes|
|5th|1d10|10 minutes|
|6th|1d10|1 hour|
|7th|1d12|1 hour|
|8th|1d12|8 hours|
|9th|1d12|24 hours|

- **The Weapon Die** applies to every hit. The die scales with slot; the number of dice never does.
- **First-hit rule.** Any _bonus_ damage beyond the Weapon Die — the Keystone's extra dice, arcs, detonations, and **every Sigil's damage die** — applies only to the first hit you land each turn.
- **Riders.** Non-damaging riders (pushes, conditions) trigger on every hit, but a creature saves against a given rider **once per turn**.
- **Bestowed.** You may draw the Infusion into a willing ally's held weapon within 30 ft instead of your own. Their hits carry it.

## Utility

_The seal does something that isn't damage, healing, a condition, or a thing: light, sight, passage, speech, shaping, finding. No dice. The Keystone decides the flavor; the slot decides the scope._

|Tier|Slot|Scope|Duration cap|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Minor**|1st–3rd|up to a 3rd-level utility effect — light, detection, unlocking, a 30-ft step, feather fall, illusion, comprehend, water breathing, clairvoyance|1 hour|
|**Moderate**|4th–6th|up to a 6th-level utility effect — flight, sending, stone shape, scrying, a 500-ft step, true seeing, wind walk|8 hours|
|**Major**|7th–9th|up to a 9th-level utility effect — teleportation across a region, mass utility, unmaking non-seal magic, a step between planes|24 hours; until countered at 9th|

_Utility seals never deal damage, heal, or impose a condition on an unwilling creature. If the effect would concentrate as a spell, it concentrates as a seal. The premium here is the seal itself — no components, no counterspell, and the Keystone's flavor._
