
The ultimate mastery of [[Cognition]] lies in the rare and powerful ability known as Arcanum Veritas, the true way of using [[Magic]]. Those who unlock this skill are able to directly inscribe the arcane glyph of a spell into the very air, creating new and unique magical effects on the fly. This was the ancient way of casting spells, predating modern magical techniques. In fact, it is believed that this method is how the gods themselves wielded their power.

### The Ban of Arcanum Veritas
___
Despite its divine origins, Arcanum Veritas was banned by the supreme god known as  [[Aeon - The God of Magic|The Architect]]. This act marked the end of an era, as the power to freely shape reality was deemed too dangerous for mortal hands. The knowledge of Arcanum Veritas faded from widespread use, restricted to divine beings and those who could still unlock its secrets through extraordinary means.

### The Witches and Forbidden Knowledge
___
Although Arcanum Veritas has long been forbidden, some creatures have managed to retain its ancient knowledge. Among the most prevalent are [[Once Upon a Star ★/Lore/Legends/Witches|Witches]]—female casters who have survived with this forbidden knowledge through the heritage of their Coven Leaders. A Witch Coven is a unique and powerful bond between a Coven Leader and her three Discipuli, young female casters who are trained in the ancient arts. When they are ready, the Discipuli will become full-fledged Witches and establish their own covens, passing on the knowledge of Arcanum Veritas to the next generation.

Yet, this knowledge comes at a great cost. Witchdom itself is banned, with a dark curse placed upon those who practice it. This curse prevents Witches from conceiving children of their own, yet it also afflicts them with an insatiable desire to have children, driving them to crave what they cannot have. This curse has been the source of endless pain and sorrow for the Witches, further isolating them from the rest of the world.

### Spell Creation with Arcanum Veritas
___
Every Arcanum Veritas is a **seal** drawn in the moment. A seal has three parts, and you choose all three when you cast:

| Part         | Ephemer term          | What it decides                                                         |
| ------------ | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Keystone** | Core Cognitions       | What the spell is — its damage type, its saving throw, its Verum Effect |
| **Ring**     | Composition           | _What shape_ it takes — a bolt, a blast, a blade, a field, a hold       |
| **Sigils**   | Complement Cognitions | _How_ it's modified — one lighter sub-effect per Sigil                  |

There are no prepared Arcanum Veritas. You draw the seal, you spend the slot, it happens.

#### Core Concepts

Verum Modifier — your highest ability score modifier. 
Seal attack bonus — proficiency bonus + Verum Modifier. 
Verum DC — 8 + proficiency bonus + Verum Modifier + Dream Score modifier. 
Saving throw type — set by the Keystone (its Main Saving Throw).

### Spell Slots and Power
___
The power of an Arcanum Veritas spell is tied to the spell slot expended to cast it. A higher-level spell slot will create a stronger, more potent effect, just as with traditional spellcasting. As a result, the caster must not only use the correct Cognitions but also sacrifice the appropriate spell slot to fuel the magic. The level of the spell slot used directly correlates with the strength of the Arcanum Veritas spell.


### Core and Complements
___
Every Arcanum Veritas is built around a **Core Cognition** — the primary concept that defines what the spell _is_. When Fire is your Core, the spell is fundamentally an act of fire; its shape, intent, and power all flow from that concept. Core Cognitions determine the spell's main damage type, saving throw, and the full range of its Verum Effects.

**Complement Cognitions** are secondary concepts woven into the spell to _augment_ it. They do not redefine the spell — they color it. A Fire spell with Ice as a Complement doesn't become a fire-and-ice hybrid; it becomes fire that leaves a chilling slowness in its wake. Complements always apply their lighter, support-oriented effects rather than their full Core expression.

The number of Complements a spell can carry is tied to spell slot level — see **Limitations** for the breakdown.


### Compositions
___
Before casting an Arcanum Veritas, the caster must declare the **Composition** — the fundamental intent of the spell. This determines how the spell scales, what it targets, and how its effects are applied. All Verum Effects from both Core and Complement Cognitions must align with the declared Composition.

For more information and see how the composition scale see [[Arcanum Veritas Composition]]

### Limitations of Arcanum Veritas
___
Arcanum Veritas casting is incredibly taxing, both physically and mentally. As such, there are strict limitations on its use:
	
- **Uses.** You may draw a number of seals per long rest equal to your **proficiency bonus**. No feature restores uses unless it says so explicitly.
- **Slot.** Each seal expends a spell slot of 1st level or higher. There are no 0-level seals.
- **Verum scaling.** Verum Effects scale by **Character Level** (see each Cognition), never by slot. The slot sets the Ring's numbers; your level sets the Keystone's tier.
- **Budget.** One Keystone. A number of Sigils equal to **the slot's level minus one** (a 1st-level seal has none; a 9th-level seal has eight).
- **Timing.** Drawing a seal is your **action**, once per turn, and can't be downgraded to a bonus action. The **Ward** Ring is a reaction; **Rite** and **Inscribed** seals take longer by design (see _Manners of Drawing_).
- **Concentration.** **Aura** fields, **Field** zones, **Control** zones, and **Constructs** require concentration. Self buffs, Ally buffs, Infusions, Structures, and Objects do not.
- **One Seal, One Roll.** A seal asks each target for **at most one d20** — either your seal attack roll or **one saving throw**. The **Keystone** sets which ability is rolled and the DC is your Verum DC; every Sigil resolves off that single result. A Sigil's own saving throw is ignored while it is a Sigil.
- **Sigil conditions are brief.** A condition imposed by a Sigil lasts **until the end of the target's next turn**. Only the Keystone's condition runs the full duration on the Ring's table.
- **Countered, not dispelled.** A seal cannot be affected by _Counterspell_ or _Dispel Magic_. It is suppressed inside an _Antimagic Field_ like any spell. The only way to unmake a seal early is an opposing seal — see Countering. Where a table says **until countered**, that is what it means.

### Countering with Arcanum Veritas
___
Spells cast through Arcanum Veritas cannot be dispelled or countered through normal magical means. Instead, they must be canceled out by another Arcanum Veritas spell that uses opposing Cognitions. For example, a spell based on the Cognition of Fire might be countered by one using the Cognition of Water, or a spell of Light could cancel out one based on Darkness.

- Core to Core: To disable a Arcanum Veritas its only necessary to have the counter Cognition of the Core Cognition. Complement Cognitions are not taken to account.
	
- Level-Based Countering: An Arcanum Veritas spell can only be countered by another Arcanum Veritas of the same spell level or higher. This ensures that more powerful Arcanum Veritas spells require equally powerful opposition.
	
- Reaction Countering: A caster can counter an opposing Arcanum Veritas spell as a Reaction. This requires knowledge of an opposing Cognition and imposes a strain on the caster if they have already used Arcanum Veritas in the same turn.
	
- Tax for Dual Use: If a caster uses Arcanum Veritas both as an action on their turn and as a reaction within the same round, they suffer 1 point of Exhaustion that cannot be removed other than sleeping.


### Manners of Drawing
___
_How the seal is drawn changes what it can do. These are not Rings — any Ring can be drawn in these manners unless noted._

#### Rite

Draw the seal over **10 minutes** instead of an action. It still spends a use and a slot. Choose one: **double the duration**, or **double the radius or size**. A Rite cannot be a Ward or a Direct Attack.

#### Inscribed

Draw the seal onto a surface, an object, or a willing creature's skin over **1 minute**. It spends a use and a slot now and waits. Set a trigger — a creature enters, touches, speaks, or a span of time passes. It fires within a number of **hours equal to the slot level**, then fades. It resolves centered on the inscription (a Targeted seal takes the triggering creature as its target). You can hold a number of inscriptions equal to your **proficiency bonus**. A Ward cannot be inscribed.

#### Coven Drawing

When you draw a seal, up to **three willing creatures** within 30 ft who know at least one Cognition may each spend their **reaction** to add one Sigil they know — beyond your Sigil budget. A Coven is a leader and three; the seal is yours, the hands are theirs.

### Arcanum Veritas Flow
___
Creating an Arcanum Veritas follows these steps in order:

**1. Declare the Spell Slot Level** — Choose the slot you are expending. This determines your total Cognition budget (1 Core + 1 Complement per level above 1st) and the base power of the spell.

**2. Choose Your Core Cognition** — Select the Cognition that defines the spell. This sets the damage type or effect category, the primary saving throw, and which Core Verum Effects are available.

**3. Choose Your Composition** — Declare the Ring: Offensive, Supportive, Control, Creation, or Utility, and its subtype (e.g. Direct Attack, Field, Ward, Structure). If the Ring has a radius, declare its **Shape** (Sphere, Cone, or Line). If you are not drawing it as a standard action, declare the **Manner** (Rite, Inscribed, or Coven Drawing). This determines how the spell scales and how it resolves.

**4. Add Complement Cognitions** — For each spell slot level above 1st, you may add one Complement Cognition. Choose the Complement's sub-effect based on the Composition you declared.

**5. Declare the Verum Effects** — Choose one Core Verum Effect and one Complement sub-effect per Complement Cognition added. All effects must be consistent with the declared Composition.

**6. Cast** — Inscribe the glyph, expend the slot, and resolve the spell. Make **one roll** — the seal attack roll, or the single saving throw set by your Keystone — and resolve the Keystone's Verum and every Sigil off that one result. If this is not your first Arcanum Veritas this round, apply the Dual Use Tax.
