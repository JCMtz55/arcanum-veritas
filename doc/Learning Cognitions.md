To learn a Cognition, a creature must **attempt a Cognition Understanding Roll** during a long rest, study session, or divine ritual. How hard that roll is — and whether dice can finish the job at all — depends on the Cognition's **Tier** (see [[Cognition Index]]).

---

## The Cognition Understanding Roll

`1d20 + Dream Score Modifier` vs. the **DC for the Cognition's Tier**.

Each Cognition has **three thresholds** of understanding. You climb them one at a time; each requires its own successful roll (or, at the highest tiers, an [[#Epiphanies (Tier III+)|Epiphany]]).

| Threshold      | Description                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Learn ¼**    | **Basic familiarity.** You may use the Cognition in limited rituals or as a **ritual component**.                                                 |
| **Learn ½**    | **Partial understanding.** You may use it as a **secondary / auxiliary Cognition** in Arcanum Veritas spells.                                     |
| **Learn Full** | **Complete mastery.** You may use it as a **primary Cognition**, create Cognition-bound items (like Cognis), and infuse it into dreamforged gear. |

---

## Difficulty by Tier

DCs are derived directly from Tier. The formula is simple:

> **Learn ¼ DC = 5 × Tier** · **Learn ½ = +5** · **Learn Full = +10**

| Tier               | Learn ¼ | Learn ½ |     Learn Full      |
| :----------------- | :-----: | :-----: | :-----------------: |
| **I — Basic**      |    5    |   10    |     15 *(roll)*     |
| **II — Advanced**  |   10    |   15    |     20 *(roll)*     |
| **III — Profound** |   15    |   20    |  **Epiphany** (25)  |
| **IV — Absolute**  |   20    |   25    |  **Epiphany** (30)  |
| **V — Primordial** |   25    |   30    | **Epiphany** (35) † |

**Tier I–II** can be fully mastered by study and dice alone.

**Tier III and higher** cannot. Dice will carry you no further than **Learn ½** — the ceiling of what can be *studied*. The final step to Full mastery requires an [[#Epiphanies (Tier III+)|Epiphany]] (see below). The number in parentheses is the DC of the roll made *during* that Epiphany.

**† Tier V** is beyond the ordinary system. [[Chaos]] and [[Order]] **cannot be learned at all** — they are taken only from beings who embody them, through narrative means. [[Imagination]] alone may be learned, but only after every other Divine Law has been fully mastered, and only through an Epiphany of divine magnitude. In practice, Tier V is not a player-facing option.

## Requirements to Attempt Learning

### 1. Access to the Cognition
To attempt a learning roll, you must meet at least **one** of the following:
- Have access to a **Cognition Source** — a raw Cognition, a **Cognitor** forged with it, a **dream inscription**, a **divine vision**, or a **Night Lord's whisper**.
- Receive **ritual guidance** from a being who knows the Cognition (a god, a Grimm, a scholar).
- Be inside a **Dream Node** or **Dream Forge** saturated with the Cognition.

### 2. Prerequisite Cognitions (Tier III+)
Profound concepts cannot be grasped without their foundations. Every Tier III+ Cognition lists **prerequisite Cognitions** in the [[Cognition Index]]. Before you may work toward one, you must already hold each prerequisite at the required depth:

| To reach... | You must hold each prerequisite at... |
| --- | --- |
| **Learn ¼–½** of a Tier III+ Cognition | **Learn ½** or better |
| **Learn Full** (via Epiphany) | **Learn Full** |

A caster cannot even *begin* to study Death (requires Decay, Flesh) without partial understanding of both, and cannot complete it until both are fully mastered. Tier IV+ prerequisites are themselves often Tier III — so the deepest Cognitions sit atop long chains of earned understanding.

## Epiphanies (Tier III+)

Some truths cannot be read, drilled, or rolled into being. A profound Cognition can be *studied* to the edge of understanding — **Learn ½** — but the leap to true mastery demands that the character not merely comprehend the concept, but **live it.** This moment is an **Epiphany.**

An Epiphany is a **narrative beat**, declared by the DM, in which the character confronts, suffers, or enacts the raw essence of the Cognition they are trying to master. It cannot be scheduled or grinded. It arrives when the story earns it.

### How it works
1. The character has reached **Learn ½** in the Cognition and holds all prerequisites at **Learn Full**.
2. When a story moment fitting the Cognition's essence occurs, the DM may declare an **Epiphany opportunity.**
3. The character makes one final **Cognition Understanding Roll at advantage**, against the Epiphany DC for that Tier (25 / 30 / 35).
4. On success, the Cognition is **Fully learned.** On a moment of sufficient narrative weight, the DM may waive the roll and grant mastery outright — the truth has already been lived.

An Epiphany may be attempted only when the moment presents itself. It is not a resource to be spent but a threshold to be *crossed.*

### Example Epiphanies
| Cognition | A fitting moment |
| --- | --- |
| **Death** | Witnessing a meaningful death, or returning from one's own. |
| **Grief** | Enduring a true, unrecoverable loss. |
| **Honor** | Being tested where keeping one's honor costs everything. |
| **Carnage** | Being consumed by slaughter — and finding the way back to oneself. |
| **Life** | Creating, saving, or nurturing life at genuine cost. |
| **Corruption** | Being truly tempted, and falling — or refusing with full knowledge of the price. |
| **Balance** | Holding two opposing forces in equilibrium at a pivotal instant. |
| **Nightmare** | Confronting one's deepest fear made flesh. |
| **Soul** | Touching, losing, or bargaining with a soul. |
| **Reality** | Watching an illusion one believed in shatter — or refusing to let it. |
| **Imagination** | *(Divine)* Authoring a new truth into the weave of the world. |

The DM should tailor the Epiphany to the character, not the checklist above — the point is that mastery of the profound is *earned in story*, never merely rolled.

---

## Restrictions & Failure Consequences

If the Cognition Understanding Roll **fails**, the consequence depends on how close it came:

| Roll relative to DC | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| **Within 2 of the DC** | You glimpse the edge of understanding. You may attempt again after **1 week**. |
| **More than 2 below the DC** | Your mind **rejects the concept.** You may **never use this same Source** to learn this Cognition again. |
| **Natural 1** | **Dreamburn:** you suffer 1 level of **Dream Exhaustion** (or psychic backlash). |

A failed **Epiphany** roll is never wasted the way a study roll can be — the moment passes, but the understanding it opened remains. The character may seek another Epiphany when the story next allows, with no Source-lockout.
